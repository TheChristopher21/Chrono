# Payroll sources and calculation scope

Identifier: `chrono-payroll-2026-v8`. Resources are pinned; runtime payroll makes no request to BMF or ESTV. Updating a year/source release requires independent fixtures and a new identifier. Never replace historical payslip amounts or saved snapshots. Unapproved earlier-version drafts must be recreated explicitly.

v8 adds actual 2026 receipts for work in **2025**, confirmed original monthly facts for that year, and separate settlements for work before a 2026 opening cutoff. Historical cash gross, tax actually withheld, insurance bases and source evidence remain unchanged; only additional amounts are charged in the new receipt document. The calculation uses the pinned 2025 BMF PAP/ESTV tariffs when tax legally belongs to 2025. Earlier work years are outside the requested scope. No 2027 tariff or general 2025 cash-payroll generation is installed. See the [v8 verification report](../../../../../docs/payroll-readiness-2026-11-06-v8.md); the v8 provisions below supersede earlier 2025/cutoff exclusions.

v7 added actual supplementary payments with independent cash, tax and social-insurance allocation; cash-only documents after exit; documented German March-clause assignment to 2025 SV; Swiss intra-month insurer/pension sections with separate UVG/KTG pools; and dated German concurrent employment using the official monthly assessment frame. Its original work/receipt scope was 2026. See the [v7 verification report](../../../../../docs/payroll-readiness-2026-11-06.md). The v7 provisions below supersede the narrower v6 limits except where extended by v8.

v6 adds independent, versioned opening balances; correction batches for used openings; contiguous canton/source-status allocations; separate KTG policy pools; hourly absence/allowance components; disclosed multiple employment; and limited post-year corrections of unchanged, already-paid 2026 gross. See the [3 November verification report](../../../../../docs/payroll-readiness-2026-11-03.md) for tested scope and remaining exclusions. No 2027 tariff is installed.

v5 aligns percentage salary contract validation with the existing 42.5-hour full-time time model (80% = 34 hours; 33% = 14.025 hours). Contractual salary hours are retained without minute rounding. The time account still rounds its modeled daily targets exactly as before. Whole-minute weekly premium thresholds remain mandatory for hourly pay. The input form accepts four decimal places. See the [2 November verification report](../../../../../docs/payroll-readiness-2026-11-02.md).

## Periods and overtime

Calendar months in 2026, including actual entry/exit partial months, completed days, dated parameters and explicit confirmation of regular monthly employment and disclosed other income are required. Salary proration is explicitly calendar-day or contractual 30-day; Swiss tax days follow KS45, including February and day-31 exceptions. Fixed BVG proration is chosen from the pension agreement (full/calendar/30-day), never inferred. Arbitrary partial periods, ages 64+, missing data and personal intra-period rule changes remain blocked. Dated insurance/pension sections, confirmed 2025 historical periods and cash-only post-exit periods are supported as described below. Only explicit supported year assignments are calculated.

Thirteenth entitlement is 1/12 of eligible base earnings, payable monthly/quarterly/semiannually/annually or on exit. Cumulative entitlement minus installments already paid avoids rounding drift and double payment. Monthly-model partial thirteenth installments are normalized to the contractual accrual cycle for rate determination. Other contractual bonus bases and ad hoc bonuses require additional rules.

The v4 annual forecast respects the contractual salary proration of a known future exit month in both countries. At Swiss year end or departure it uses actually paid, rounded thirteenth installments. Annual source-tax reconciliation aggregates the taxable base by historical tariff before rounding, then subtracts tax already withheld. Removing the thirteenth entitlement is blocked while past earned installments remain unsettled; the engine does not invent a settlement schedule.

Swiss snapshots retain contractual KTG caps and optional separate UVG/KTG policy pools. Same-pool caps must agree. A new insurer has its own insured days and ceiling; ALV remains continuous. Older snapshots without an explicit cap can infer it only from the exact same immutable insurance version, otherwise chronological correction is required. Historical JSON remains readable. See the v7 section for intra-month boundaries and fixed BVG allocation.

Hourly pay uses canonical minutes once; weekly excess over contract hours receives the agreed premium. Preceding days of a split calendar week are included, with unchanged rules across that week. Salary pay has no automatic 160-hour threshold. Explicit overtime uses whole minutes, contract basis/premium, entitlement earned by period end and current availability. Percentage employment uses time-account weekly rounding. Conflicting contract/time-model hours block payout. Unsupported CH statutory-overtime premium assumptions are rejected. [Swiss overtime overview](https://www.ch.ch/de/arbeit/arbeitszeit/uberstunden-und-uberzeit/).

## Corrections and compatibility

Rückrechnungen create separate signed difference documents linked to an approved original and its previous revision. The selected period and all approved following financial periods in the same year are recalculated chronologically in one transaction; complete batches are approved together. Hash references, current configuration/time-data recalculation and user locks reject stale/competing runs. Approved originals, earlier corrections and stored PDFs are never rewritten or removed. Negative overtime deltas return paid minutes to the time account. A correction repairs the original payroll period; actual new cash is recorded separately through the v7 receipt-period input described below.

V53 only adds nullable correction metadata, indexes and non-cascading references. V54/V55 add opening/period-input version tables and nullable insurance-pool fields; V56 adds nullable UVG pool fields, without updating historical payslips. Existing v2 approved snapshots remain readable. Legacy hours-only slips are excluded from new financial history before an explicit opening cutoff, while their time-account deductions remain effective. Openings require complete actual months, historical terms, explicit gross/insurance/tax bases, thirteenth entitlement and a confirmed source. Empty amounts are unknown, never zero. A used opening is revised through a staged version and complete correction batch; activation and approval are atomic. Discarded/stale versions cannot silently replace the active basis. Legacy component/net discrepancies are shown as separate reconciliation positions; overlapping financial originals block automatic correction. Old unapproved snapshots must be recreated; they are not upgraded in place.

Approval and correction generation use current locking reads for approved history, terms, insurance/pension, openings and period inputs after the employee lock, including MySQL REPEATABLE READ transactions that opened an older snapshot before waiting. Overtime balance calculations under that lock use the same current payout history. A German correction changing a non-monthly thirteenth payment in another payout month is blocked unless the unchanged gross was demonstrably paid in its original periods; genuinely new payments require actual receipt-month allocation.

Hourly contracts may specify separately disclosed vacation/holiday percentages or payment on absence. Cash vacation compensation requires explicit legal/contractual confirmation (minimum four-week rate 8.33%; age/GAV entitlement must be checked by the employer). Versioned period inputs supply actual absence minutes, evidenced average rates and employer sick-pay percentage. Component totals are rounded once; contradictory allowance plus payment of the same absence is rejected. Recorded leave/holidays require a confirmed monthly entitlement review; an explicit documented no-payment result is distinguishable from missing information. Direct insurer benefits are not employer wage continuation. These payments do not rewrite clock data.

Swiss disclosed multiple income uses known total percentage, normalization to 100% where the total is unknown, or known other periodic income including its thirteenth share. Only periodic income is scaled; other aperiodic receipts require explicit per-month values. German main/secondary ELStAM class is checked, and monthly SV ceilings are allocated using employer/fund-confirmed other earnings under [SGB IV §22](https://rvrecht.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/SharedDocs/rvRecht/01_GRA_SGB/04_SGB_IV/pp_0001_25/gra_sgb004_p_0022.html). Dated unequal entry/exit periods use the official monthly assessment frame described in v7 below. One-off SV allocations require explicit fund-confirmed bases and cannot exceed remaining own headroom.

Post-year corrections of already-paid 2026 gross require unchanged gross, source evidence, the complete actual year/exit, and creation/approval by the following 31 March. Germany additionally requires an untransmitted certificate and a legally permitted annual adjustment by the last February payroll ([§41c](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__41c.html), [§42b](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__42b.html)). The supported German annual case is a full uninterrupted year with stable tax/insurance characteristics, classes I–IV, no factor/allowances/additions or multiple employment. Independent annual BMF fixtures in `bmf-2026-year-end-reference.json` cover four synthetic cases; collector `tools/payroll/collect_bmf_year_end_fixtures.py` uses no employee data. New receipt-year 2027 remuneration and rewriting prior-year original gross remain blocked. A genuinely new 2026 receipt for 2025 work is a separate v8 settlement, not an overwrite of that original gross.

## Supplementary payments and insurance sections (v7)

Actual new payments are recorded in versioned receipt-period inputs with a unique annual reference, kind, actual work/entitlement/receipt dates, gross and confirmation that associated thirteenth entitlements are fully settled. Cash is paid once in the receipt period. For work in 2026, German regular arrears retain their original work-period tax/SV; other remuneration uses receipt-period tax and the applicable SV assignment, including the last employment month after exit. Swiss SV for 2026 work uses the original work period; source tax follows receipt rules during employment and KS45 6.9/7.5.3 after exit, distinguishing vested rights from new rights arising after exit. Cash-only periods cover the remainder of the exit month or a full later month without new wages, employment days, thirteenth entitlement or overtime deductions. A changed plan requires a complete chronological correction batch from the earliest affected original period before the cash period can be approved. Future receipts, duplicate references and stale drafts are blocked. Original amounts, snapshots, PDFs and time deductions are retained; new financial correction chains do not silently adopt later hours-only legacy documents.

German first-quarter one-off receipts use the actual receipt month for the March clause, even if SV belongs to the last employment month. If the whole payment must be assigned to 2025, the employer supplies the original assignment date, insurance days, assessed RV/KV bases, health-fund rate, nursing-insurance status, source and confirmation; multiple employment also requires fund-confirmed allocated bases. Prior-year headroom is consumed once and stored separately from 2026 ceilings. The 2025 annual limits are RV EUR 96,600 and KV EUR 66,150; in v7 these were SV reference data only. v8 adds the separate historical PAP and may derive the prior-year facts from complete confirmed original monthly records. Missing original data blocks the run. Four additional independent BMF program-test cases in `bmf-2026-payments-reference.json` cover ordinary arrears and monthly/daily one-off taxation after exit. Collector: `tools/payroll/collect_bmf_payment_fixtures.ps1`; synthetic inputs only.

Swiss insurance/pension boundaries require actual SV gross and agreed employee/employer BVG amounts for every exact contiguous coverage section. The engine does not guess daily wage allocation or a changed pension agreement. If the pension version is unchanged, section totals must match its contractual amount. Separate UVG insurers have separate ceiling pools: unused headroom cannot transfer to a new insurer. ALV remains continuous over the employment year. Contiguous sections within the same pool are merged before Swiss 30/360 counting, including rate changes on day 31. Saved snapshots capture individual section dates, policy versions, pools, caps, assessed bases and BVG amounts. V56 adds only nullable UVG pool fields and never updates historical payslip amounts.

For German dated concurrent jobs, connected overlapping employment periods determine the official monthly assessment frame; each employer's actual wage is capped before proportional allocation. Disjoint jobs do not consume this employment's ceiling. This replaces the v6 identical-period restriction; it does not assume equal daily wages. The independent published GKV example 12 (2014 limits supplied explicitly in the test) verifies the unequal entry/exit calculation.

## Historical work and opening-cutoff settlements (v8)

The employer supplies complete, versioned **actual original 2025 monthly records**, from January/entry through December/exit: gross components, thirteenth entitlement, insurance days, assessed bases and tax actually withheld, including signed refunds and canton attribution where needed. Original dated personal terms, company insurance/pension versions, explicit prior-employer gross and concurrent-employment facts are resolved independently. These facts create no cash payment or clock deduction. Missing records are not replaced by current salary or assumed rates.

For Germany, already-owed regular 2025 wage is assigned to the original 2025 SV period, using the 2025 monthly RV/KV caps EUR 8,050/EUR 5,512.50 and its actual insurance status. Under [LStH R39b.2/R39b.5](https://ao.bundesfinanzministerium.de/lsth/2026/A-Einkommensteuergesetz/VI-Steuererhebung-36-47/2-Steuerabzug-vom-Arbeitslohn-Lohnsteuer-38-42g/Paragraf-39b/r-39b-2.html), receipt through 21 January 2026 retains the 2025 regular tax period; later receipt is 2026 other remuneration. A retroactive 2025 wage increase newly agreed in 2026 has a separate `RETROACTIVE_RAISE` kind: regular SV belongs to the agreement month or the last employment month if the agreement follows exit, following the [DRV wage-arrears guidance](https://rvrecht.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/SharedDocs/rvRecht/01_GRA_SGB/04_SGB_IV/pp_0001_25/p_0014/gra_sgb004_p_0014_gehaltsnachzahlungen.html). It is not silently treated as an ordinary bonus. New one-off receipts after a 2025 exit consume 2025 SV headroom only when their actual receipt is in the first quarter. Historical regular employee SV recovery requires explicit confirmation that the additional deduction is permitted under [§28g SGB IV](https://rvrecht.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/SharedDocs/rvRecht/05_Normen_und_Vertraege/01_Sozialgesetzbuch/04_SGB_IV/pp_0026_50/0028g/0028g_2009_07_01.html); the engine does not assume every omitted employee contribution can be recovered.

Later regular 2025 arrears also change the 2025 headroom available to earlier 2026 March-clause payments. Such earlier approved financial periods must first be corrected chronologically. Repeated receipts settle the cumulative difference less amounts already charged; historical bases do not consume current 2026 ceilings. German historical SV differences use the difference between rounded original/new contributions, rather than rounding an increment alone.

For Switzerland, the [BSV WBB 2025](https://sozialversicherungen.admin.ch/de/d/6923/download?version=20), Rz 2036–2039, distinguishes the work-year liability from receipt-year realization: 2025 arrears during continued employment use the 2026 receipt-year SV assessment; after the employment has ended, historical work-year insurance rules/bases apply. Vested source-tax rights after exit use the last employment tax period and its 2025 tariff, crediting tax actually withheld; new rights after exit use the 2026 receipt period and the applicable rate-determining income (KS45 6.9/7.5.3). Additional employee/employer BVG amounts require explicit confirmation per historical payment, including zero. Original monthly BVG or coverage-section contributions are not deducted a second time. Intra-month historical insurance changes require both original and revised actual section wages.

For work before a 2026 opening cutoff, imported original figures remain immutable. Revised tax/SV bases are carried in the new calculation history, while the original paid tax is credited only once. Any affected approved new financial periods must adopt those bases through a complete correction batch before the receipt can be approved. The separate receipt document discloses original/revised bases and historical deduction/contribution differences; no extra cash gross, employment days, automatic thirteenth entitlement or overtime debit is invented. A new cash-payroll start may follow an earlier exit, provided imported periods cover the actual employment through that exit. A documented historical tax balance can result in a negative net settlement; the PDF labels it as a repayment/offset requiring separate handling, not a bank payment order. Ordinary unexplained negative payroll remains blocked.

Not automated: work years before 2025; 2027 payroll or new 2027 receipts; general 2025 cash-payroll generation; negative new-payment amounts (use explicit corrections); ad hoc bonuses with other contractual bases; general wage-replacement benefit assessment; retirement exceptions; mini/midi jobs; private/special insurance; cross-border exceptions outside supported tariffs; country/insurance-status changes outside the stated historical cases; and ELStAM/ELM filings. Historical AHV liability changes from work performed before AHV age require separate treatment and are blocked. Missing company policy, BVG, ELStAM, fund-confirmed and personal-status data remain blocking requirements. These tests do not constitute Swissdec certification or statutory filing support.

Revised regular tax gross is carried into later imported non-monthly thirteenth taxation. For 2026 opening periods, native social-payment grouping retains the actual receipt quarter and separates March-clause 2025 headroom from later 2026 one-offs. An original March-clause thirteenth already present in an opening requires the original allocated prior-year bases as dated inputs. That original-opening case combined with multiple employment remains blocked until separate original/revised collection-agency allocations are available.

New v8 documents without overtime effects never rebuild the time account on approval, repeated approval or draft reopening; actual overtime payouts and legacy approval behavior remain intact. This is checked across all approval routes. The browser verification report also records the actual source-2025 Swiss settlement, repeated-payment headroom, and preservation of old documents.
