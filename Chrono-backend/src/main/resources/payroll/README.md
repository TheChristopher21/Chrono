# Payroll sources and calculation scope

Identifier: `chrono-payroll-2026-v3`. Resources are pinned; runtime payroll makes no request to BMF or ESTV. Updating a year/source release requires independent fixtures and a new identifier. Never replace historical payslip amounts or saved snapshots.

## Germany

- Original [BMF XML 2026](https://www.bmf-steuerrechner.de/javax.faces.resource/daten/xmls/Lohnsteuer2026.xml.xhtml): `Lohnsteuer2026`, version 1.0, dated 2025-10-23, retrieved 2026-10-16.
- File `de/Lohnsteuer2026.xml`; SHA-256 `63d8981646d139eba2f4dd990c13b43c4fb3883b402a5a40cddf253aa7aa96b4`.
- From the repository root: `python tools/payroll/generate_bmf.py`. The mechanical translator rejects unknown XML statements. Do not hand-edit the generated class.
- Independent fixture `src/test/resources/payroll/bmf-2026-reference.json`: 15 synthetic results obtained from the official BMF program-test interface, not generated Java. Optional collector: `tools/payroll/collect_bmf_fixtures.py`. The published interface code is for program tests only, never runtime payroll or real employee data.
- Eight additional independent BMF cases in `bmf-2026-extended-reference.json` cover daily taxation and other remuneration; collector `tools/payroll/collect_bmf_extended_fixtures.py`. Partial employment months use daily PAP results multiplied by calendar days; SV uses at most 30 actual days. Contractual monthly thirteenth installments are ordinary pay; less frequent installments use SONSTB with projected annual ordinary earnings, previous paid installments and explicit prior-employer wages. Future one-off payments are excluded. See [LStH 2026, R 39b.6](https://ao.bundesfinanzministerium.de/lsth/2026/A-Einkommensteuergesetz/VI-Steuererhebung-36-47/2-Steuerabzug-vom-Arbeitslohn-Lohnsteuer-38-42g/Paragraf-39b/inhalt.html). Annual SV headroom is tracked separately for pension and health insurance. U1/U2 exclude one-off installments. [March-clause cases](https://www.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/DRV/DE/Experten/Arbeitgeber-und-Steuerberater/summa-summarum/Lexikon/M/maerzklausel.html) requiring prior-year SV data are blocked.
- The wrapper supports monthly ordinary statutory insurance above EUR 2,000, manually supplied ELStAM values, full health-fund additional contribution and explicit employer levy rates. RV/ALV monthly ceiling EUR 8,450; KV/PV EUR 5,812.50. [Official 2026 ceilings](https://www.bundesregierung.de/breg-de/aktuelles/beitragsgemessungsgrenzen-2386514), [BMG nursing contributions](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/themen/pflege/online-ratgeber-pflege/die-pflegeversicherung/finanzierung).
- Church tax uses PAP assessment basis and the supplied 0/8/9% rate. Special minimum/capping rules are not implemented. No ELStAM retrieval, statutory filings or employer accident-insurance annual assessment is provided. [BMF calculator scope](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Standardartikel/Service/Abgabenrechner/FAQ.html).

## Switzerland

- [ESTV import publication](https://www.estv.admin.ch/de/quellensteuertarife-import-in-lohnbuchhaltungssysteme), [2026 archive](https://www.estv2.admin.ch/qst/2026/loehne/tar2026txt.zip), release listed 2026-03-04, retrieved 2026-10-16.
- File `ch/tar2026txt.zip`; SHA-256 `619b53e364545ffcd32e01669983326f79e5dea82c520de5e158c4e9e10943d6`. All 26 cantons are included; calculation supports the 21 monthly-model cantons and annual-model FR, GE, TI, VD and VS.
- [Record format](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/AvojuyFCZY95/qst-tarife-recordformate-loehne-2025-de.pdf): record 06, rate in ten-thousandths, amounts in cents, dates/canton checked. Missing variants are errors, including JU A0N; no fallback to A0Y.
- [ESTV FAQ, circular 45](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/a7vG5hlZeAdW/dbst-ks-2022-1-045-d-faq-de.pdf): floor rate-determining income to whole francs for lookup; round final tax to CHF 0.05. Independent boundary comparison: [Zurich A without church tax 2026](https://www.zh.ch/content/dam/zhweb/bilder-dokumente/themen/steuern-finanzen/steuern/quellensteuer/quellensteuertarif/2026/p208840_QuellensteuerTarif_A_ohne_KS_ENT1.pdf), A0N CHF 5,000 at 5.52%, CHF 5,001 at 5.59%.
- [AHV/IV/EO and ALV 2026](https://www.ahv-iv.ch/p/2.01.d): applicable birth year, AHV/IV/EO 5.3% per side, ALV 1.1% per side. The cumulative ALV/UVG ceiling is CHF 148,200 per 360 insurance days, less already assessed bases. Contractual KTG cap is explicit; zero means unlimited. Missing year-to-date bases block ceiling adjustments rather than inventing opening balances.
- [ESTV circular 45](https://www.estv.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/I3YyTvU3ThVA/dbst-ks-2019-1-045-d-de.pdf), sections 6.3/6.6 and 7.2–7.3: annual salary forecast includes a known contractual thirteenth entitlement from the outset. Each run recalculates preceding periods at the revised annual income using their historical tariff codes, then subtracts tax already withheld. Aperiodic overtime is not annualized. This follows the circular, not the separate Swissdec realization-only method for thirteenth salaries. Complete approved history since January/entry is mandatory. Changes of canton/source-tax status require separate year allocation and are blocked.
- Independent annual-band references: [Vaud 2026 table, p. 20](https://www.vd.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/organisation/dfin/aci/fichiers_pdf/21034-10_Employeurs_2026.pdf), A0 CHF 60,000 at 8.17%, CHF 60,001 at 8.34%; [Geneva 2026 table](https://www.ge.ch/document/41802/telecharger), A0 CHF 59,999 at 7.49%, CHF 60,000 at 7.62%. Geneva uses lower-inclusive x,000 annual bounds; the other four use x,001. The pinned Geneva initial zero-rate interval's five-cent gap contains no whole-franc lookup value.
- Calculated Swiss components use commercial CHF 0.05 rounding: [Swissdec payroll guidelines v5](https://swissdec.ch/document/share/358/ccfd0c26-59fd-495b-8c20-6b3435a7c383). Fixed salary/BVG inputs retain supplied cents. This is not Swissdec certification or ELM filing.
- Ordinary Swiss income tax is not a payroll deduction. Each employer supplies dated BU/NBU/KTG/FAK, AHV administration and individual BVG amounts; no statutory substitute is invented.

## Periods and overtime

Calendar months in 2026, including actual entry/exit partial months, completed days, dated parameters and explicit confirmation of the regular single-employment case are required. Salary proration is explicitly calendar-day or contractual 30-day; Swiss tax days follow KS45, including February and day-31 exceptions. Fixed BVG proration is chosen from the pension agreement (full/calendar/30-day), never inferred. Arbitrary partial periods, ages 64+, missing data and intra-period rule changes remain blocked. No extrapolation to another year.

Thirteenth entitlement is 1/12 of eligible base earnings, payable monthly/quarterly/semiannually/annually or on exit. Cumulative entitlement minus installments already paid avoids rounding drift and double payment. Monthly-model partial thirteenth installments are normalized to the contractual accrual cycle for rate determination. Other contractual bonus bases and ad hoc bonuses require additional rules.

Hourly pay uses canonical minutes once; weekly excess over contract hours receives the agreed premium. Preceding days of a split calendar week are included, with unchanged rules across that week. Salary pay has no automatic 160-hour threshold. Explicit overtime uses whole minutes, contract basis/premium, entitlement earned by period end and current availability. Percentage employment uses time-account weekly rounding. Conflicting contract/time-model hours block payout. Unsupported CH statutory-overtime premium assumptions are rejected. [Swiss overtime overview](https://www.ch.ch/de/arbeit/arbeitszeit/uberstunden-und-uberzeit/).

## Corrections and compatibility

Rückrechnungen create separate signed difference documents linked to an approved original and its previous revision. The selected period and all approved following periods in the same year are recalculated chronologically in one transaction; complete batches are approved together. Hash references, current configuration/time-data recalculation and user locks reject stale/competing runs. Approved originals, earlier corrections and stored PDFs are never rewritten or removed. Negative overtime deltas return paid minutes to the time account. A correction is a repair of the original payroll period; this is not a general engine for newly granted retrospective remuneration with a different tax receipt period.

V53 only adds nullable correction metadata, indexes and non-cascading references. Existing v2 approved snapshots remain readable. Unknown legacy annual bases require explicit chronological correction documents before further annual calculations. Legacy component/net discrepancies are shown as separate reconciliation positions; overlapping approved originals block automatic correction. Old unapproved snapshots must be recreated; they are not upgraded in place.

Not automated: paid absence components/holiday allowances in hourly pay, ad hoc bonuses, multi-employment/replacement income, retirement exceptions, mini/midi jobs, private/special insurance, cross-border exceptions outside supported tariffs, prior-year SV allocations and cross-year corrections. The UI names the scope and requires the employer to confirm that unrepresented cases do not apply. Extending scope requires explicit inputs and independent reference cases. These tests do not constitute Swissdec certification or statutory filing support.
